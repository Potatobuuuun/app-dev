# Favourite Series: Arcane: League of Legends
## Created by Riot Games
### Animated by Studio Fortiche
#### Status: Finished Airing (November 23, 2024)
##### Seasons: 2
###### Number of Episodes: Season 1 = 9, Season 2 = 9

Season 1 Plot:
1. Act I: "The Base Violence Necessary for Change" (Episodes 1–3)
   1. Introduces the divide between Piltover and Zaun. After a failed rebellion, Vander adopts orphaned sisters Vi and Powder. A botched heist leads Powder to steal volatile blue crystals, causing a massive explosion that separates the sisters. Jayce and Viktor begin experimenting with the crystals, laying the foundation for Hextech.
  2. Act II: "When These Walls Come Tumbling Down" (Episodes 4–6)
     2. Years later, Piltover thrives on Hextech innovation while Zaun suffers under Silco’s rule. Vi is imprisoned, and Powder grows increasingly unstable. Jayce and Viktor face ethical dilemmas with their work. Silco kidnaps Vander to manipulate Powder into helping him, deepening the conflict.
  3. Act III: "The Monster You Created" (Episodes 7–9)
     3. Powder, traumatized and manipulated, kills Vander and her brothers during a failed rescue, leading Vi to reject her and call her "Jinx." Silco takes Jinx in. In the climax, Jinx kills Silco during a confrontation and launches a rocket at the Piltover Council, destroying hopes for peace.
        
Season 2 Plot:
1. Act I: "Finally Got the Name Right" (Episodes 10–12)
   1. Set immediately after Season 1, Zaun descends into chaos without Silco. Jinx retreats but returns after a child she befriended is killed. Vi and Caitlyn pursue her, while Viktor struggles with deteriorating health. Mycroft investigates the Council bombing.
  2. Act II: "The Message Hidden Within the Pattern" (Episodes 13–15)
     2. Vi and Jinx discover Vander is alive, transformed into the beast Warwick. They seek Viktor’s help, but tensions escalate. Mel Medarda and Jayce face political fallout. Jinx’s mental state worsens as she grapples with identity and trauma.
  3. Act III: "The Dirt Under Your Nails" (Episodes 16–18)
     3. The sisters confront their fractured bond amid rising violence. Viktor’s condition worsens, pushing him toward radical transformation. The season culminates in a final battle that reshapes Piltover and Zaun, resolving the sisters’ conflict and setting up the future of the League of Legends world.
